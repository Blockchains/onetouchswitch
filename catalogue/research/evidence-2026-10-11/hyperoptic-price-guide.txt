Introduction
Our Guide to Charges and Fees for Residential
Customers is designed so that you can quickly
view and understand our packages and pricing.

If you’d like further information or answers to any
questions you may have regarding our products
and pricing, please contact our Customer
Support in any of the ways set out under “Our
Contact Details” below.

The prices set out in this Guide are our normal
charges, but your order for our services is subject
to any speciﬁc pricing or applicable special
promotion that was communicated to you during
your order process and conﬁrmed in your order
conﬁrmation email.
