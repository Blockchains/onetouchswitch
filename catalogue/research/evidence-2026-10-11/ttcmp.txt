Compare Deals FAQs (7 items)
Can I get Full Fibre broadband?

Full Fibre and Part-Fibre availability varies by area. Pop in your postcode to check what’s ready to go at your address here.

How can I compare the best broadband deals?

Start with your household needs, then compare speed, contract length and monthly price. Our comparison tools keep it simple — and show you the best value in your area.

What speed is right for my home?

Light browsing? Small household? Fibre 35 or 65 might be plenty. Big family? Heavy streaming? Go Full Fibre for nextlevel stability and speed.

Do I need a landline?

Not necessarily. Many of our broadband-only deals work without a landline, depending on your address.

Which TalkTalk broadband deal is best for my household?

It depends on the size of your home, the speed you need and how many devices you use. Our personalised recommendations will point you in the right direction.

How do I find broadband deals in my area?

Just enter your postcode here and we’ll show you every TalkTalk deal available where you live.

Will I lose my broadband when I switch?

With One Touch Switch, you stay connected while the changeover happens behind the scenes. Easy, stress-free, and no downtime for most customers.
