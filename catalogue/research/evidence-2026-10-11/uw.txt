What is Full Fibre broadband?

Full Fibre, or Fibre to the Premises (FTTP), is revolutionising the world of broadband. 

Traditional Fibre to the Cabinet (FTTC) broadband transmits data to a cabinet on your street, and old copper cables take it the rest of the way to your home. With FTTP, fibre cables send internet data directly to your home. With Full Fibre, you’ll get lightning-fast broadband speeds and a more reliable connection, and you won’t have to share any bandwidth with your neighbours. 

As one of the quickest internet options available today, Full Fibre is being rolled out across the UK, and is slowly phasing out FTTC broadband.
