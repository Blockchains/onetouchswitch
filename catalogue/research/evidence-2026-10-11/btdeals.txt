HOW TO SWITCH TO BT WITH ONE TOUCH SWITCH

One Touch Switch is quick and hassle-free. Just tell us you want to switch from when you order, and we'll do the rest. You won't have to let your current provider know you're leaving them. We'll sort everything for you.

Pick a broadband package that suits your needs

Start by choosing one of our great value broadband or home phone deals.

We'll contact your current provider

Tell us your current provider and we'll inform them that you're switching to us. They'll contact you about any changes to your services or charges.

We'll activate your BT Broadband

Once you've placed your order, we'll take care of the switch. Your current service will stay on until your BT Broadband is ready.
