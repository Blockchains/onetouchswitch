Any questions?
These are the most frequently asked
Is Hyperoptic available where I live?

Enter your postcode to see if Hyperoptic Full Fibre is available at your address. If it is, you can place your order online in just a few minutes. If we’re not there yet, you can register your interest and we’ll keep you updated when we’re coming to your area.

Why is Full Fibre better than standard broadband?

Unlike older broadband that relies on copper cables, Hyperoptic’s Full Fibre network uses fibre all the way to your home. That means faster speeds, a more reliable connection and consistent performance, even when everyone in the household is online at the same time.

How fast is Hyperoptic broadband really?

Our Full Fibre broadband offers speeds of up to 1Gbps, making it ideal for streaming in 4K, online gaming, video calls and busy households with multiple connected devices. Whatever you do online, Hyperoptic is built to keep up.

Can I switch without losing my connection?

Yes. Switching to Hyperoptic is simple, and we’ll guide you through every step. Depending on your current provider, we can even handle the switch for you, helping to minimise disruption and make getting connected as smooth as possible.

Do I get a Wi-Fi router included?

Yes. Every Hyperoptic broadband package comes with a Wi-Fi router included at no extra cost, so you’ll have everything you need to get online from day one.

Can I upgrade my speed later?

Absolutely. If your needs change, you can easily upgrade to a faster broadband package. Whether you’ve added more devices, started working from home or simply want more speed, you can choose the package that’s right for you.

Can I keep my home phone number?

Yes. If you choose one of our home phone services, you can usually transfer your existing landline number to Hyperoptic. We’ll let you know what’s possible when you place your order and guide you through the process.

Why do customers choose Hyperoptic?

Customers choose Hyperoptic for our fast, reliable Full Fibre broadband, excellent customer service and straightforward pricing. Whether you’re streaming, gaming, working from home or simply browsing, you can enjoy a connection that’s built to keep up with modern life.

Minimum download speed & Expected Wi-Fi speed

We provide a minimum download speed that you can expect on a package, at any time of day, when connecting one device to the router (and that connection is wired). If the speed you receive is slower than it should be, please contact us, we’ll check the problem and try our best to fix in line with our Minimum Download Speed Guarantee.

Expected Wi-Fi speeds shown are derived from supplier testing data when connecting one device to the router over wi-fi, and can vary depending on distance from router, home environment and number of devices connected. Your speed will be shared between all the connected devices. For optimal experience, we recommend using a wired connection to the Hyperoptic router, choosing a higher speed or using a mesh system like Hyperoptic’s Total Wi-Fi.
