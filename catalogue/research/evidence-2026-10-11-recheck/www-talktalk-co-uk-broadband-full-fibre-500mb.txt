Frequently Asked Questions (8 items)
Is 500Mbps broadband fast enough for gaming and 4K streaming at the same time?

Yes. Full Fibre 500 is designed to support demanding online activities across multiple devices simultaneously, including gaming, 4K streaming and HD video calls.

Is 500Mbps considered fast broadband?

Absolutely. With average download speeds of 500Mbps, Full Fibre 500 is significantly faster than standard broadband and designed for households with high internet usage.

What is the current price of TalkTalk Full Fibre 500?

Full Fibre 500 starts from £30 per month, increasing to £33 from April 2026 and £36 from April 2027.

Does Full Fibre 500 require engineer installation?

Yes. Full Fibre broadband requires an engineer installation to connect your home directly to the Full Fibre network.

What's the difference between Full Fibre 150, 500 and 900?

The main difference is speed. Full Fibre 500 offers a balance between premium performance and value, making it ideal for larger households, gamers and heavy internet users.

Do I need a phone line for Full Fibre 500?

No. Full Fibre 500 is a broadband-only service and does not require a traditional phone line.

What is Wi-Fi 6?

Wi-Fi 6 is the latest generation of Wi-Fi technology included with the TalkTalk Wi-Fi Hub 3. It helps improve performance, efficiency and connectivity across multiple devices.

What is Total Home Wi-Fi?

Total Home Wi‑Fi is a solution designed to help improve wireless coverage around your home and reduce Wi‑Fi blackspots.
