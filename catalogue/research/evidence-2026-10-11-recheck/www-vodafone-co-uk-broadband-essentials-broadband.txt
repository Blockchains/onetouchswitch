About Vodafone Essentials

At Vodafone, we understand the importance of staying connected through these difficult times. To help out, we have designed Vodafone Essentials Broadband. This is an affordable broadband plan from just £20 a month (66p a day).

Our 12-month contract gives you the certainty of no in-contract price rises but the flexibility to leave with no early termination fees. Available for all existing and new customers who are receiving specific government benefits.
