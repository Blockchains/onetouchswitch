WHAT SPEED DO I NEED?

For most homes, You 200 or You 1000 handles streaming, working from home and multiple devices comfortably. Go higher if you've got serious gamers or a lot of smart-home kit. Find out more

IS SWITCHING A HASSLE?

We'll handle it. We tell your old provider you're leaving so we can seamlessly switch you over on install day, no downtime. You can be up and running in 10 days, or earlier if your diary is free. Find out more

ROLLING OR FIXED CONTRACT?

A rolling contract is flexible, with no commitment, but prices can change. A fixed one locks your price for 12 months, so there's no nasty surprises. Find out more.

I'M STILL IN CONTRACT
