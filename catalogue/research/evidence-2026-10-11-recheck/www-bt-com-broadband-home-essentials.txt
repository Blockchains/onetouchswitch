WHO CAN GET BT HOME ESSENTIALS?

New or existing BT customers can apply. You’ll also need to be receiving one of these benefits to qualify:

Universal Credit (all claimants)
Pension Credit (Guarantee Credit)
Employment and Support Allowance
Jobseeker’s Allowance
Income Support

Don’t forget you’ll need your National Insurance number when you order, so we can check you’re eligible. Additional checks are in place if you apply to our BT Home Essentials No Income plan - see the BT Home Essentials T&Cs.
