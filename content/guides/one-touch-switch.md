Under Ofcom's **One Touch Switch** process, which launched for most customers on **12 September 2024**, you usually only need to contact your **new** provider. It arranges the switch with your old one, including between different networks.

## What the rules give you

- You do not pay notice-period charges for your old service after the switch date.
- Providers must compensate you if the switch goes wrong or you are left without service for more than one working day.
- Early termination charges for leaving a minimum term early can still apply. Include them in your comparison.

## What we do and don't do

We help you compare total costs. **We do not switch you and we do not take orders.** When you have chosen a deal, check availability and sign up with the provider.
