Most of the UK is served by Openreach's network, which many national providers use. **Hull and parts of the East Riding of Yorkshire are different.** They are mainly served by KCOM's own network, and other full-fibre networks are being built there.

## What this means for you

- Many national providers that rely on Openreach may not be available at your address.
- Choice depends heavily on your exact address.

When your postcode is in Kingston upon Hull or the East Riding of Yorkshire, we show a notice. We never hide offers based on a guess. Always check availability at your exact address with the provider.
