## Postcodes

We use **OS Code-Point Open**, an Open Government Licence dataset of all GB postcodes, split into small files by outward code and served with this site. Looking up a postcode only downloads the file for its outward code, such as HU1, from this site. No postcode service is called.

Contains OS data © Crown copyright and database right; Royal Mail data © Royal Mail copyright and database right; National Statistics data © Crown copyright and database right.

## Offers

Offers appear only after a person has reviewed and approved them, and they are published as a numbered, versioned catalogue. If nothing is published, we tell you so and you can compare quotes you've been given. We never make up offers.

## Privacy

- No account and no email address needed.
- Calculations run in your browser.
- Saved comparisons stay in this browser and keep only the first half of your postcode (for example HU1), never your address.
- Bills are read on your device and never uploaded.

## Works offline

Once this site has loaded, it keeps working without a connection: postcode lookups for areas you've used or kept offline, manual address entry, costing, guides and saved comparisons.
