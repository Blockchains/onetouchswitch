The fastest way to fill in "Your current deal" is from your latest bill.

## Option 1: read a PDF bill on this device

Choose a PDF or text bill. Your browser reads the text **on this device**: nothing is uploaded or stored. We look for your provider, monthly price, bill total, contract end date and exit fee. We show the line each value came from, and you confirm or correct each one before we use it.

## Option 2: paste or type

Copy the text of your bill and paste it in, or type the four numbers into the form. You get exactly the same comparison.

## Photos and scanned bills

Reading text from a photo needs OCR, which is too heavy to run well in a static web page. So for now, type the values in. We don't send your bill to any service.
