Many UK providers offer **social tariffs**: cheaper broadband for people who get certain benefits, such as Universal Credit or Pension Credit. They usually cost much less than standard deals, and many have no exit fee.

## Who can get one

Each provider sets its own rules. Common qualifying benefits include Universal Credit, Pension Credit, Jobseeker's Allowance, Employment and Support Allowance, and Income Support.

## How to get one

- Ask your current provider. You can often move to its social tariff without paying an exit fee. Check with the provider.
- Ofcom keeps a list of available social tariffs.
- When you get a price, add it here as a quote to see the total cost against your current deal.

Source: [Ofcom: Social tariffs](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/phones-and-broadband/saving-money/social-tariffs)
