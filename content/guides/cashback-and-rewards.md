---
title: Cashback, vouchers and rewards
summary: We show cashback but never take it off the price.
tags: [cashback, voucher, reward, gift card, incentive]
updated: 2026-10-11
---
Deals often come with cashback, vouchers or gift cards. They can be worth having, but:

- You often have to **claim** them, sometimes months later, and they can have conditions.
- They are **not a price**. Your monthly bill stays the same.

So we **never deduct** them from the cost. We show them as a note under the result, so you can weigh them up yourself.
